# Example 3 — A fake mockup and stat band become real evidence

## Before

Hero section, as generated:

- A browser-frame mockup containing a blurred, generic analytics
  dashboard — not this product, which has no analytics view.
- Below it, a stat band: "10,000+ customers · 99.9% uptime · 4.9/5
  rating". No source, date, or methodology exists for any of the three
  figures; the product has 340 paying customers and no uptime
  instrumentation yet.

Every element is decoration dressed as evidence: the mockup fabricates a
feature, the band fabricates a track record.

## After

Hero section, corrected:

- The mockup is replaced with a current screenshot of the product's real
  dispatch board, at readable resolution, unframed.
- The stat band is replaced with what can be sourced: "340 dispatch
  teams plan routes here daily — billing export, March" and a named
  customer quote with their real company and role. Uptime is omitted
  until instrumentation exists; when it does, the figure ships with its
  measurement window and source.

The numbers are smaller. They are also true, and the screenshot shows the
thing a buyer is actually evaluating.

Note: corrects FE-007 — the frontend renders real data only; a claim the data cannot support is removed, not decorated.
