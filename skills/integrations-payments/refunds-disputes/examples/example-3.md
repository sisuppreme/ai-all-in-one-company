# Example 3 — The dispute answered on deadline day

## Before

A dispute-created webhook arrives, but nothing consumes it — no case, no alert, no owner. The dispute surfaces three weeks later when someone opens the provider dashboard and sees the response window closes that evening. The team assembles evidence in a rush, uploads a folder of loosely related documents, and submits minutes before the deadline with no review. The issuer rules against them, and the amount plus the dispute fee stay debited.

## After

The dispute-created webhook opens a case the same day: named owner alerted, provider deadline and dispute reason recorded, and an internal deadline set days earlier. The evidence pack is assembled against the reason checklist — receipt, access logs, terms acceptance, communication record — passes a completeness review, and is submitted once, before the internal deadline, because submission cannot be edited afterward. The case then tracks provider events to a real won/lost closure.

*Demonstrates IN-048 / IN-049 / IN-051 — same-day ownership, an internal deadline ahead of the provider's, and a reviewed one-shot evidence submission.*
