# Example 1 — A copy-first fact corrected to sheet-first

## Before

The About page ships with: "Founded in 2019, we now serve dispatch teams in
14 countries."

The fact sheet contains no founding date and no country count. Both numbers
were written into the layout first and never verified; the country count came
from counting rows in a spreadsheet of trial signups, most of them inactive.

## After

Process correction, in order:

1. The claims come off the About page the same day.
2. The fact sheet gains two entries, each with evidence:
   - Founding: incorporated March 2019 — evidence: certificate of
     incorporation, filed with the sheet.
   - Service area: paying customers in 6 countries as of the March billing
     export — evidence: billing configuration report, linked.
3. The About page is republished from the sheet: "Founded in 2019, we serve
   dispatch teams in 6 countries."

The published numbers are smaller. They are also true, traceable, and stable
across every page that copies them.

Note: corrects PR-121 — a fact enters copy only after it exists in the fact sheet with its evidence.
