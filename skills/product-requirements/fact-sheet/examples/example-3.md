# Example 3 — Replacing a plausible fill with "not measured"

## Before

Fact sheet, reliability section: "Uptime: 99.9%."

Nobody measured it. The figure was entered because the template had a field
and "99.9%" is what such fields usually say. The pricing page later copied it,
and a customer quoted it back during an outage.

## After

Fact sheet, reliability section: "Uptime: not measured. No uptime monitoring
is in place as of the March review; a status probe is scheduled before the
next review date. Until then, no uptime figure may appear in copy."

The pricing page's uptime line is removed the same day, and the sheet's
history records the change, the date, and the reason.

Note: corrects PR-126 — a field with no data states "not measured"; a plausible fill is a fabrication.
