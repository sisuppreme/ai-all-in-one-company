# Example 3 — Assumption list with the riskiest assumption tested first

## Before

Solution committed to the sprint: "Build a live countdown on the home screen."

No assumptions recorded. The sprint discovers in week two that most riders open
the app only after reaching the stop — the countdown solves a moment that
rarely happens.

## After

Candidate solution: live countdown on the home screen.

Assumptions:
- Desirability: riders check the app before leaving for the stop. (Important,
  no evidence — RISKIEST.)
- Usability: riders can read the countdown at a glance outdoors.
- Feasibility: the vehicle feed updates at least every 30 seconds.
- Viability: no new data license is required.

Test attached to the riskiest assumption: interview five riders this week,
asking for the story of the last time they caught the bus; record when they
opened the app relative to leaving. Build starts only after the result is on
the tree.

Note: corrects PR-006 and PR-007 — every candidate solution carries an explicit assumption list, and the most important, least-evidenced assumption is tested first.
