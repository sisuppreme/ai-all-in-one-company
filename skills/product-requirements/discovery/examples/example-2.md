# Example 2 — Recording evidence as a specific-moment story

## Before

Evidence entry: "Users find reporting confusing and want it improved."

No participant, no moment, no task. Nothing downstream can cite this, and
nothing in it can be checked.

## After

Evidence entry (interview, dispatcher, March 3 session): "Yesterday the
month-end totals were missing from the export, so I downloaded the CSV three
times and added the column by hand before sending it to finance. It took about
an hour."

The story names a person type, a specific past moment, the task, and the cost —
an opportunity ("month-end totals are missing from the standard export") can be
mapped from it.

Note: corrects PR-004 — interview evidence is recorded as stories about specific past moments, not summarized opinions.
