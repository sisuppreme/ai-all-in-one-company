# Example 1 — Framing an opportunity as a need

## Before

Opportunity node on the tree: "Add push notifications."

The node names a build task. Any solution comparison is meaningless, because the
"solution" was chosen when the node was written.

## After

Opportunity node on the tree: "Riders miss their bus because they do not know it
is two stops away."

Candidate solutions attached under it: push notification on approach, a live
countdown on the home screen, an SMS alert for saved routes. Each is compared
and assumption-tested before one enters the backlog.

Note: corrects PR-002 — opportunities are framed as customer needs, never as solutions.
