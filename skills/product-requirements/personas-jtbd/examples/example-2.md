# Example 2 — Building a persona from findings instead of biography

## Before

Persona: "Dana, 38, dispatch manager. Married, two kids, loves hiking.
Frustrated by slow software. Wants tools that just work."

Every line was invented in one sitting. No finding supports any of it, and the
"frustration" is generic enough to justify any feature.

## After

Persona: Dispatch lead (day shift) — v1.0, evidence date March 7, built from
findings F-09, F-11, F-14 (study S-03).

Job: When the day's routes are published, I want every late order visible in
one place, so I can rebalance drivers before the first customer calls.

Representative tasks (in participants' words): "check what's already late";
"move a job to another driver"; "send the month-end totals to finance."

Constraints from findings: works from a shared office PC, not a personal
device (F-09); exports to spreadsheets when on-screen totals disagree with
finance (F-11).

No name, age, or hobbies — no finding supports them, and the job does not
change without them.

Note: corrects PR-045 and PR-049 — the persona is evidence-linked and versioned, and its tasks are drawn from observed findings.
