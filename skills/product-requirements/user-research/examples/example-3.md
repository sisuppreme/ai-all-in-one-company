# Example 3 — A claim that carries its citation and its limits

## Before

Roadmap justification: "Users want mobile access, so the mobile app is a Must
have this quarter."

No finding, no count, no limits. The claim cannot be checked and quietly
becomes a commitment.

## After

Roadmap justification: "Dispatchers finish reports away from their desks
(F-11, S-03: 7 of 9 exported to spreadsheets, 4 of them then worked from a
phone). Corroboration: support tickets tagged 'mobile' — 23 in February, from
11 distinct accounts. Limit: night-shift dispatchers were not reached (S-03),
so this claim covers day operations only."

The claim names its findings, its second source type, and the population it
does not cover.

Note: corrects PR-024 and PR-025 — no claim without a cited finding, and sample limits are stated as part of the claim.
