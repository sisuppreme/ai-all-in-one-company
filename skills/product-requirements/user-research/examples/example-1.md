# Example 1 — A complete study entry

## Before

Study: "User interviews — people liked the new export."

No method detail, no participants, no dates. This entry can support any claim
and therefore supports none.

## After

Study S-03 — Method: semi-structured interviews, 45 minutes, remote.
Participants: 9 dispatchers from 4 customer companies, recruited from the
support-ticket requester list. Dates: March 3–7.
Not reached: night-shift dispatchers (all sessions ran in daytime hours) —
findings do not cover night workflows.
Raw records: notes stored with this study, one file per participant (P-01…P-09).

Note: corrects PR-022 — every study entry records method, participant count and source, and dates.
