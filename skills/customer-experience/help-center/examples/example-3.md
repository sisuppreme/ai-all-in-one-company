# Example 3 — Promise restated in an article footer

## Before

Every article ends with a hand-written footer: "Still stuck? Email us — we reply within 4 hours, 24/7!" Support is one person working business hours, so the promise is false on most days and users cite the footer when replies take a day.

## After

The footer is replaced everywhere with: "Still stuck? [Contact support](link) — see our current support hours and expected reply times." The hours and reply-time expectation live only on the configured support-expectations page, which the operator updates in one place.

Note: corrects CX-049 — support promises are linked from the single configured page, never restated in article text.
