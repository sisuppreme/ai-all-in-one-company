# Example 2 — Article born from a zero-result search

## Before

The monthly search report shows 14 searches for "download my data" returning no results. Nobody reviews the report, so the queries keep failing and several users open tickets asking whether export is possible at all.

## After

The zero-result report is reviewed on the cycle; the repeated query becomes a new article, "Export and download your data," written from the ticket answers agents were already giving, titled in the customers' phrasing and tagged with "download my data" as a search term. The next report shows the query resolving to the article.

Note: corrects CX-047 — repeated zero-result searches are work items that become new or fixed articles.
