# Example 2 — Cancellation maze

## Before

Starting the trial took one click from the pricing page. Cancelling means finding a settings page, clicking "Manage plan," dismissing two retention modals ("Are you sure? You'll lose everything"), and finally being told to "contact support to complete your cancellation" — support that replies within two business days, after the trial has already converted and charged.

## After

The trial settings page has a "Cancel trial" action beside the trial end date. One click opens a single screen with an optional reason question and a "Confirm cancellation" button. Confirming ends billing immediately, shows "You will not be charged. Pro access continues until 4 December 2026," and sends a confirmation email. Total steps: fewer than signup.

Violates/corrects CX-108 — cancelling takes no more steps than joining, completes in-product, and always completes.
