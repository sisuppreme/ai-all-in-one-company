# Example 3 — The footer claim

Note: demonstrates LG-125 (public claims are in scope) and BAN-LG-062.

## Before

The marketing site's footer carries a badge: "Fully GDPR & SOC 2 Compliant." The claims scan (G-LG-062) flags it: neither phrase is on the approved-claims list. Checking the audit file, the DSAR control has a passing drill dated this quarter, but there is no SOC 2 report at all — the product has never undergone a SOC 2 audit — and no artifact supports the word "fully" for any regime.

## After

The badge is removed the same day and filed as a finding with an owner and date. In its place, the site publishes only what the evidence proves, worded as what was tested, when, and against what: "Data-subject request flow tested end to end against our obligations register, November 2026 — audit record available on request." The SOC 2 wording returns only if and when an actual SOC 2 report exists to support it, and then inside that framework's own scope (LG-126).
