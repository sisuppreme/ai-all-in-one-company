# Example 3 — The template that kept the brand

## Before

```text
Delivery note: "Rebuilt competitor pricing page as a reusable template —
pixel-perfect." The rebuild ships with the competitor's logo in the header,
its headline and plan copy verbatim, its product photography, and its color
scheme and page dress unchanged. G-LG-094 residue scan: 31 hits (brand name,
slogan, 14 harvested image hashes) — none on an approved-reuse list.
```

A live brand cloned end to end — logo, copy, and photography kept — and called a template.

## After

```text
Delivery note: "Pricing page recreated from the reference's layout pattern:
three-tier table, annual/monthly toggle, comparison grid — rebuilt in original
code." The rebuild carries the client's own logo and branding, freshly written
plan copy from the fact sheet, and licensed-or-owned imagery. G-LG-094 residue
scan: 0 hits. Side-by-side, no viewer could mistake it for the source brand.
```

## Note

Violates LG-186 and LG-187 (protected expression and brand identity are never replicated for a competing product; patterns may be recreated, identity may not); corrected by rebuilding the pattern with original branding, copy, and imagery.
