# Example 2 — The compliant badge

## Before

```markdown
<!-- pricing page, trust row -->
★ GDPR Compliant   ★ Your data stays private   ★ Trusted by 2,000+ teams
```

The badge ships with the landing page. Behind it there is no obligations register, no DSAR flow, no retention schedule, and nobody has tested a single control — the claim is decoration, and it is the first thing a regulator or a customer's counsel will ask the company to prove.

## After

```markdown
<!-- pricing page, trust row -->
Data handling described in our Privacy Notice (v3, 2026-11-21).
Obligations register: 14 purposes, each with a recorded lawful basis and
retention period — last verified 2026-11-21, available on request.
Access and erasure requests: handled through Settings → Privacy,
median response recorded in the request log.
```

Every sentence names an artifact that exists, a date it was verified, and a flow a visitor can actually use. Nothing claims "compliance"; the wording states what was built and when it was checked, which is all the evidence supports.

## Note

Corrected under LG-011: the skill never declares a product "GDPR compliant"; public wording is limited to what a dated artifact can prove (see also BAN-LG-001).
