# Example 3 — Out-of-scope pages kept as excluded rows

## Before

The engagement covers only the marketing site, so the drafter deletes 48 discovered rows for the help center and the legacy support portal from the inventory draft. Months later the client asks why the help center was "missed," and nothing in the deliverable can show it was ever seen, let alone deliberately excluded.

## After

All 48 rows stay in the table with scope status `excluded` and reasons: "help center — separate engagement," "legacy portal — retired, capture retained." The signed scope contract counts only `built` and `planned` rows, and the excluded rows make the boundary of the engagement auditable instead of arguable.

Note: corrects CL-026 — no row is deleted silently; out-of-scope pages remain in the inventory as excluded rows with a stated reason.
