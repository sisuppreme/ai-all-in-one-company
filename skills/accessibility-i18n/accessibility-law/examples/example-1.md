# Example 1 — "ADA compliant" badge vs. technical result

## Before

Homepage footer and sales deck, first slide:

> "Our platform is 100% ADA compliant and fully accessible to all users."

No standard, version, level, scope, or date is named anywhere. No audit has been run for the current release, and the claim was written by marketing from a competitor's website.

## After

Homepage footer links to the accessibility statement; the sales deck states:

> "Evaluated against WCAG 2.2 Level AA on 2026-11-21 (audit report A11Y-2026-11, scope: marketing site and signup flow). Known exceptions are listed in the accessibility statement. Legal applicability for specific jurisdictions is confirmed by D14 on request."

Note: the bare compliance verdict is replaced by the dated technical result — standard, version, level, scope, date — per AC-065.
