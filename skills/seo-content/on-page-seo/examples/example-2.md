# Example 2 — Two pages, one intent, resolved by differentiation

## Before

A new page is published targeting "invoice template" while an existing page already owns that intent. The two pages are near-identical — same structure, same download, different hero image — and they split clicks in Search Console while neither ranks well.

## After

The intent map records a written differentiation before publication: the existing page keeps the primary intent "invoice template" as the downloadable template library, and the new page is re-scoped to the distinct intent "how to write an invoice" — a step-by-step guide with a worked example — with its title, H1, and outline rewritten to match, and an internal link from the guide to the library.

Note: corrects CO-021 and CO-028 — one primary intent per page, and intent collisions resolved in writing (merge, redirect, or differentiate) instead of shipping a near-duplicate (BAN-CO-014).
