# Example 1 — Benefit filler rewritten with a mechanism

## Before

> Unlock the power of seamless collaboration and revolutionize the way your team works. TaskBoard helps you take your projects to the next level with an intuitive, all-in-one platform designed for modern teams.

## After

> In TaskBoard, a comment on any task can be turned into a follow-up task with one click, keeping the decision and the work in the same thread. Boards show each task's owner and due date without opening it, so a standup starts from the board, not from memory.

Note: Demonstrates CO-101 — every benefit sentence in the "after" names a mechanism (comment-to-task conversion, owner/due-date on the board face) a competitor cannot print verbatim, replacing category filler that named none.
