# Example 2 — Hedge stack and cluster terms edited to a plain claim

## Before

> In today's fast-paced world, it's important to note that TaskBoard may potentially help your team to possibly improve how it might generally track its work. Furthermore, teams can delve into a rich tapestry of features that could sometimes streamline communication.

## After

> TaskBoard puts every task's owner, due date, and status on one board. Status changes notify the task's watchers immediately, so nobody has to ask for an update in chat.

Note: Demonstrates CO-103 and CO-104 — the cluster terms ("in today's fast-paced world", "it's important to note", "delve", "tapestry", "furthermore") are removed, and the five-qualifier hedge stack is replaced by verified, plainly stated facts about what the product does.
