# Example 3 — Superlative and statistic cut to substantiated copy

## Before

> TaskBoard is the #1 best-rated project tool, trusted by over 10,000 teams who ship 87% faster. Join the leading platform for modern project management today.

## After

> TaskBoard is a project board for small teams: tasks, owners, due dates, and file attachments in one place, with a free plan for up to three projects. Pricing and plan limits are listed on the pricing page and charged exactly as shown at checkout.

Note: Demonstrates CO-105 and CO-106 — the "#1 best-rated" superlative, the "10,000 teams" counter, and the "87% faster" figure had no ranking, dataset, or fact-sheet entry behind them, so all three were cut and replaced with verifiable product facts; had a survey or dataset existed, the claim could have stayed with its source named.
