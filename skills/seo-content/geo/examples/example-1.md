# Example 1 — GEO plan claims tagged by evidence level

## Before

A GEO plan lists its tactics as flat facts:

> - Deploy llms.txt so AI engines can read the site.
> - Add statistics and quotes — proven to boost AI visibility by 40%.
> - Build brand mentions: a confirmed AI ranking factor.
> - Expected result: brand cited in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers within 60 days.

## After

The same plan, with every claim tagged and the guarantee removed:

> - llms.txt: **[hype as a ranking tactic]** Google states on the record that it does not use the file; may ship as hygiene, never reported as a result.
> - Add statistics and quotations with named sources: **[peer-reviewed, limited]** Aggarwal et al. (KDD '24) measured visibility gains up to ~40% for these methods on 2023-era models in a controlled benchmark — an upper bound, not an expected average.
> - Build genuine brand mentions: **[correlational]** vendor studies associate mentions with AI Overview presence; no platform states this as a factor. Treated as a hypothesis the prompt panel will test.
> - Measurement: fixed prompt panel run monthly per engine; results reported as panel aggregates with variance. No citation outcome is promised.

Note: corrects CO-061, CO-071 — every GEO claim carries its evidence level, and no outcome is guaranteed.
