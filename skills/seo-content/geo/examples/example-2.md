# Example 2 — Statistic made citable with provenance, not invention

## Before

A guide page is "optimized for citation" by adding an impressive figure:

> Teams that adopt a launch checklist ship 43% faster and cut rollback incidents in half, according to industry research.

No study is named, none is held on file, and the figure appears nowhere in fact-sheet.md.

## After

The claim is rebuilt from evidence the project actually holds:

> In this project's own release log for March–October 2026 (fact-sheet.md, "Release data"), the 14 releases that used the launch checklist needed a rollback once; the 11 releases without it needed rollbacks four times. The full log is published with the guide.

The number is smaller and less round — and it is real, checkable, and safe for an engine to quote.

Note: corrects CO-065, CO-066 — citable content uses original data with stated provenance; an invented statistic is a DET-CON-011 blocker even when added to earn citations.
