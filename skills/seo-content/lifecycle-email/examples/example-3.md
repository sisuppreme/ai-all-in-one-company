# Example 3 — Deceptive subject and invented count replaced

## Before

A promotional email in the engagement journey ships with:

```
From: Sarah from Example Co
Subject: Re: Your refund is ready
Body: Join 50,000 happy customers who upgraded this month — your
      exclusive 40% discount expires tonight!
```

There is no Sarah, no refund, and the business has 900 accounts; the discount has no end date.

## After

The same email rewritten to state only what is true and sourced:

```
From: Example Co
Subject: 40% off the Team plan until Friday — what's included
Body: The Team plan is 40% off until Friday, 21 November. It adds
      shared workspaces and priority support to your current plan.
      [Unsubscribe in one click — footer]
```

The customer count is cut entirely until a figure exists in fact-sheet.md, and the discount's real end date replaces the invented urgency.

Note: corrects CO-150 (honest subject and sender — no fake "Re:", no invented person) and BAN-CO-073 (no statistic or social-proof claim without a source record).
