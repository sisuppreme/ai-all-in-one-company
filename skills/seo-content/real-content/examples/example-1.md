# Example 1 — The testimonial with no customer

## Before

> "TaskBoard cut our planning time in half. It's the first tool the whole team actually opens."
> — Sarah M., Marketing Manager
>
> ★★★★★

The quote was written to fill the social-proof section. There is no customer named Sarah M., no email, review, or signed quote on file, and the five stars were typed, not collected.

## After

The section ships with the testimonial removed and the space given to a verifiable product fact from fact-sheet.md:

> TaskBoard boards keep every task, owner, and due date on one screen — no status meeting required to find out where work stands.

When a real customer later provides a quotable endorsement, it is added with its source record logged in the evidence register.

*Demonstrates CO-122 / BAN-CO-061: a fabricated testimonial is cut, never softened or kept as a placeholder.*
