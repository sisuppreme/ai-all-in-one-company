# Example 3 — The promise with nothing behind it

## Before

Pricing page footer: "Every plan includes 24/7 support and a 30-day money-back guarantee."

Support is actually email-only on weekdays, and no refund policy exists anywhere in the project's documents. The sentence was written because the layout "needed reassurance."

## After

The sentence is replaced with the terms that are actually true and documented:

Pricing page footer: "Every plan includes email support on business days, answered within one business day. See our [refund policy](/refund-policy) for cancellation and refund terms."

A refund policy page is published first, so the link resolves to a real backing artifact; the support-hours claim matches the documented support process.

*Demonstrates CO-125 / BAN-CO-064: no backing artifact, no promise — state the real terms and link the policy that honors them.*
