# Example 2 — The counter that disagreed with itself

## Before

Homepage hero: "Trusted by 10,000+ teams."
About page: "More than 12,000 teams plan their work in TaskBoard."

Neither figure has a fact-sheet entry, and the two pages disagree — each was written independently, months apart.

## After

The count is pulled from the product's own account records, entered in fact-sheet.md with its source system and as-of date, and both pages render the same canonical figure from that entry:

Homepage hero: "Trusted by 10,000+ teams."
About page: "More than 10,000 teams plan their work in TaskBoard."

The figure is re-checked against the source system at each scheduled fact-sheet review.

*Demonstrates CO-123: a first-party counter needs a fact-sheet entry and must agree everywhere it appears.*
