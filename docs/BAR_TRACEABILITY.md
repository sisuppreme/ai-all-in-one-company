# BAR_TRACEABILITY.md — Quality Bar → gate → evidence

Ship gate criterion 9 (brief §9.1): every Quality Bar (QB-01…QB-14, brief §6) maps to the gate(s) or mechanism that test it, the department or skill that owns enforcement, and the evidence that it passed. The Evidence column is updated as evidence lands — an entry that says "pending" is the honest state, not a gap to be edited away. Most bars are demonstrated on the Phase 6 proof projects (P1 TaskBoard, P2 Atlas Blog, P3 Ledger API, P4 ShopClone); Phase 6 evidence landed 2026-11-21 and is recorded below and in full in `QA_REPORT.md` (repo root). Phase 7 evidence is the final document set (README.md, USER_GUIDE.md, RESEARCH_PAPER.md, WORKFLOW.md, QA_REPORT.md), produced in Phase 7.
